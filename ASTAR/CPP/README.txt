NHOM 03 | A* - CPP
========================================

FILE
- astar.cpp : ma nguon thuat toan A*.
- input.txt : do thi, heuristic, chi phi canh va trang thai dau/dich.
- output.txt : duoc tao/cap nhat khi chay, gom bang cac buoc, duong di va chi phi.
- RUN_ASTAR.bat : bien dich va chay tren Windows.

CHAY TREN WINDOWS
Chay RUN_ASTAR.bat. May can co Dev-C++/MinGW GCC.

CHAY THU CONG
  g++ -std=c++11 astar.cpp -o astar.exe
  astar.exe input.txt output.txt
